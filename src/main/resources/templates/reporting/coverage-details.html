How these details match the total

The credited overlap starts at the latest of the session start, assigned start, and selected period's start. It ends at the earliest of the session end, assigned end, and current time. Breaks and your own assigned windows do not count.

Pauses and resumes appear as separate recorded sessions. Overlapping time is counted once, then rounded down to whole minutes for the total. The breakdown shows seconds so small contributions remain visible. Each service type on a date counts as one shift after at least one minute of coverage, even across multiple windows or sessions.

Full coverage means the entire assigned window is covered within the selected period. Coverage creates no points, penalties, or priority changes for future assignments.
