How rankings are calculated

Ranked by whole recorded minutes. Equal totals share a rank; drivers with no whole minutes are unranked. Each start/resume creates a segment, so segment count does not determine rank.

Breaks and scheduled assignments do not add time. Active sessions include elapsed time up to the four-hour limit; manager time corrections are reflected here. This week starts Monday. All dates use .

Coverage is tracked when you start or resume driving. When the run ends, only the time overlapping another driver's assignment counts. Your own assignments and unassigned gaps do not count as covering for others. Each service type on a date counts as one shift helped cover after at least one minute, even if you pause and resume.

Coverage requires an assignment saved when the run began; older runs without that information still count toward total driving time. Runs awaiting a time review count toward coverage after review, and corrections update coverage totals. Coverage time is already included in driving time. It creates no scheduling bonus or deduction; normal actual-hours balancing is unchanged.

These team totals are available to signed-in drivers and managers in your organization.
