Upload the employee schedule

Use the exported Dayforce CSV or Excel (.xlsx), up to 5 MB. Uploading again replaces demand and driver work hours for the work dates in that file. Registered drivers are matched by employee ID.

File format

Upload CSV or XLSX from Dayforce. Report headings above the table and multiple schedule worksheets are supported.

Required columns: Employee ID, Employee Name (or Employee Full Name), Shift Start, and Shift End. The full “Scheduled Shift Start Date Time” and “Scheduled Shift End Date Time” headings also work.

Excel date/time cells are supported. Text dates should look like 10/24/2026 9:00 AM. Unscheduled employees with both times blank are skipped. Identical shifts are counted once across worksheets. Replace formulas with values before uploading.
