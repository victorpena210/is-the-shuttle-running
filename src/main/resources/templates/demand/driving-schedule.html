How assignments work

Assignments use park hours and saved driver availability for the next 28 days. Each day starts with its own driving balance. Past total hours stay in the driving history and do not create make-up assignments. Available drivers share the day’s driving time, and equally balanced plans rotate duties based on who actually drove them on previous days. Midday opening and scare-actor transport stay together when one driver can cover both. Future days rotate from the preceding plan until actual driving is recorded.

Tap the shuttle NFC/QR checkpoint when you start or stop driving. A scheduled shift does not start the shuttle or add driving time.

Recorded driving shows actual logged times, including dates with no saved schedule, and reflects manager corrections.

Opening service requires another scheduled roster member at the wardrobe counter. For scare-actor transport, a sole available driver may temporarily close wardrobe and reopen on return. Evening service runs through park closing or the day's later scheduled work finish. Each driver stays within their saved work hours. Employee-demand uploads can add pickup windows and are optional.

Adjust wardrobe hours

An 11 AM or noon park opening uses a 9 AM–noon shuttle run. A 5 PM park opening uses a 3–5 PM shuttle run. During Fright Fest, the same available driver continues with scare actors from 5 PM until finished (about 5:45 PM). Opening service requires another counter worker; a sole available driver may temporarily close wardrobe for the scare-actor run and reopen on return. Regular opening service does not resume after the scare-actor run.

A daily setting overrides the wardrobe opening time. Morning opening service lasts up to three hours; afternoon opening service lasts up to two hours. Both stop earlier if wardrobe closes. Midnight closing means the end of this day.
