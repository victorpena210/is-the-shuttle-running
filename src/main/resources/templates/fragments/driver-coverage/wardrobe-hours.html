Wardrobe hours

Save an opening time below to use a three-hour opening window that moves with wardrobe hours.

On afternoon-start days with no morning demand, the first demand period is opening coverage: one rostered driver drives while another covers the counter until wardrobe closes. Other midday and evening driving wait for demand at or after closing. Regular midday service remains optional.

An existing driving assignment conflicts with demand, wardrobe hours, or opening counter coverage. Review it below; its history or manager decision has been preserved.
Set seasonal hours

Choose the season dates and the days this schedule applies. A weekday rule overrides an every-day rule; a one-day override takes priority over both.

Change this day only

A daily override takes priority over seasonal hours. It affects only the selected date.

Friday example: choose Fridays, open 3 PM and close 7 PM for a 3–6 PM opening window. Saturday example: open 9 AM for a 9 AM–noon window. Later service begins with demand after closing.

Dates without a matching season use 7 PM. Midnight means the end of the selected day. Hours are saved for this organization; changing them updates upcoming automatic assignments.
