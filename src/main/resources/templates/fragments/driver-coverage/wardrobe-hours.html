Wardrobe hours

Midday and evening driving start with the first demand window at or after wardrobe closes, subject to driver availability. Midday remains optional. Morning driving still requires a second roster member to cover the office.

An existing midday or evening assignment starts before eligible demand or wardrobe closing. Review it below; its history or manager decision has been preserved.
Set seasonal hours

Choose the dates for the season. A newer seasonal rule replaces older rules on overlapping dates.

Change this day only

A daily override takes priority over seasonal hours. It affects only the selected date.

Dates without a matching season use 7 PM. Midnight means the end of the selected day. Hours are saved for this organization; changing them updates upcoming automatic assignments.
