How NFC and QR driving records work

Tap the NFC tag or scan the QR when a driving stretch starts. Use either checkpoint method again when you need to pause or end. If you resume later in the same assignment, RoutePulse opens another actual driving session and keeps the earlier one in the record.

If you cover for another scheduled driver, RoutePulse keeps the original schedule intact and records you separately as the actual driver.

An active driver is never silently replaced. RoutePulse shows a handoff confirmation before ending the previous actual session and starting the replacement driver's session.
