Start, cover, and end driving

Use the permanent QR code posted at the shuttle. RoutePulse identifies you from your signed-in account, not from the QR code itself.

Your first valid scan: starts your assigned shift. If another driver was scheduled but has not started, RoutePulse keeps the original assignment intact and records you separately as the actual fill-in driver.

Your next valid scan while driving: ends only the current actual driving session and saves its end time.

If service resumes during the same scheduled shift: scan again. RoutePulse reopens that assignment with a new actual driving session, so breaks, accidental END scans, and temporary shuttle downtime remain visible in the history.

RoutePulse will never automatically replace a driver who has already started driving. If another driver has an active session, a manager must resolve it first.
