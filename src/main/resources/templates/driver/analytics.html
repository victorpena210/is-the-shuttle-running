How these numbers are calculated

Driving time comes from your recorded start and stop times. Pauses are excluded. Overnight sessions are split across local dates, and overlapping records count only once in total time. A session spanning midnight appears in both days’ session counts but only once in the overall count.

Days with driving includes dates with recorded driving time. The average divides total time by those days, excluding days with no driving. Durations are rounded down to whole minutes; sessions under a minute are shown as <1 min.

Rider confirmations are optional QR submissions attributed to you when submitted. They are not a complete passenger count; zero confirmations does not mean you did not drive.

Only your actual driving is included. Being assigned a shift does not add driving time. These figures reflect retained records and are not a payroll or attendance calculation.
