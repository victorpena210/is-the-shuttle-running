A few helpful answers

Before you
come aboard.

Is this only a shuttle-status page?

Riders see whether service is active and the driver’s first name. Drivers use NFC or QR checkpoints, and managers review actual driving records.

Do riders need an account?

No. Riders open the organization’s public link to check status. While service is active, they can also confirm a ride without providing a name, employee ID, or email address.

Does it show the shuttle on a GPS map?

The current product shows service status based on driver activity and operational updates. It does not provide live GPS tracking or predicted arrival times.

What happened to RoutePulse?

RoutePulse is now Is the Shuttle Running? The app focuses on actual driving records and a clear live status for riders.
