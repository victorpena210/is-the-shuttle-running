One operating workflow

From schedules to completed trips.

RoutePulse turns the weekly workforce schedule into a practical daily shuttle plan, then records what actually happened during service.

01

Upload workforce schedules

Import the latest employee schedule so RoutePulse knows who is working and when.

02

See transportation demand

Turn employee shift times into morning arrivals, midday turnover, and evening pickup demand.

03

Assign driver coverage

Build driver coverage around actual transportation demand, work schedules, availability, and fair driving distribution — with manager overrides when operations change.

04

Verify actual operations

Compare planned service with NFC/QR driving activity, live status, passenger evidence, fill-ins, breaks, pauses, and schedule changes to build a reliable record of what actually happened.
