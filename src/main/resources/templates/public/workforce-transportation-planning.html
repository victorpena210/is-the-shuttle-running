Planning for exceptions, not just the ideal day

Employee transportation changes when schedules are revised, operating calendars are incomplete, drivers call out, or the organization is open for a special event. Is the Shuttle Running is structured so those exceptions can be reviewed explicitly rather than hidden inside a spreadsheet.

  • Schedule-change reconciliation preserves the prior assignment and explains what changed.
  • Calendar verification and authorized overrides give managers a documented way to handle unusual operating days.
  • Planning dates can be reviewed historically instead of treating the dashboard as today-only.
  • Demand and driver coverage remain separate concepts, which makes gaps easier to identify.

Workforce transportation planning FAQs

How does Is the Shuttle Running calculate transportation demand?

Is the Shuttle Running uses employee shift timing to identify expected arrivals, departures, and midday movement. The goal is to show the operational demand created by the workforce schedule.

What happens if the schedule changes after it was already uploaded?

A newer upload can be reconciled against the existing plan. Is the Shuttle Running preserves the original decision, explains the change, and lets a manager accept a recommendation or keep the current assignment.

Can a manager plan for a special operating day?

Yes. Is the Shuttle Running supports documented calendar overrides so an authorized manager can confirm an operating day when an external calendar is unavailable or incorrect.
