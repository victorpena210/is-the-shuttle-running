Driver coverage

Schedule shuttle drivers around real demand and real availability.

RoutePulse helps managers build coverage without losing the distinction between who was scheduled, who filled in, and who actually drove.

Core principle

Scheduled responsibility belongs to the planned driver. Actual driving belongs to the person who drove. RoutePulse keeps both.

Direct answer

How does RoutePulse handle shuttle driver scheduling?

RoutePulse builds driver coverage from the transportation window, each driver's work schedule, and workload rules. Managers can override an assignment when operations require it, while the system preserves the planned driver and records actual driving separately.

Coverage that understands more than a name on a calendar

Schedule availability

A driver should only be recommended when their employee work schedule meaningfully overlaps the shuttle coverage window.

Fairness and workload

RoutePulse can use prior workload and make-up priority so repeated coverage does not quietly fall on the same person.

Manager control

Automatic planning remains advisory. Authorized managers can assign a different driver and record why the operational decision changed.

Actual-driver tracking

If another driver covers the work, the scheduled driver stays intact while actual sessions identify the person who physically drove.

What happens when a driver misses coverage?

RoutePulse can review a completed assignment outcome and apply future make-up priority when a scheduled driver missed or had someone else cover the assignment. That makes the fairness model operational instead of merely reporting total hours after the fact.

For midday coverage, RoutePulse can also work with more specific start and stop windows rather than assuming every driver has one continuous morning or evening block.

Shuttle driver scheduling FAQs

How does RoutePulse choose a driver?

RoutePulse evaluates the coverage window against driver work schedules and its workload rules, then produces a planned assignment for manager review.

Can a manager override the automatic assignment?

Yes. Managers can replace the suggested driver without deleting the original planning history.

What if somebody else drives instead?

The scheduled assignment remains intact. RoutePulse records the person who actually drove in the driving-session history so planned and actual responsibility do not get mixed together.

Can a missed assignment affect future driver priority?

Yes. A reviewed missed or covered assignment can create future make-up priority so the workload can be rebalanced intentionally.
