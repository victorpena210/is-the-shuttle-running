Direct answer

What is RoutePulse?

RoutePulse is web-based employee shuttle operations software. It converts workforce schedules into expected transportation demand, helps managers plan driver coverage, records actual driving through operational checkpoints, and compares planned service with what occurred in the field.

What RoutePulse manages

RoutePulse is designed for the operational layer between an employee schedule and a completed shuttle day. Instead of keeping demand, driver assignments, service status, and records in separate tools, the product keeps those decisions connected.

Plan from shift data

Import the latest workforce schedule and turn employee start and end times into practical morning, midday, and evening transportation demand.

Build driver coverage

Assign drivers using availability, planned demand, workload distribution, make-up priority, and manager overrides when real life changes the plan.

Operate the service

Track whether service is running, paused, delayed, or unavailable while preserving the difference between the scheduled driver and the person who actually drove.

Verify the operation

Use QR/NFC checkpoints, driving sessions, schedule context, rider confirmations, and management review to build an auditable service record.

Who is RoutePulse for?

RoutePulse is for organizations that operate employee shuttle service around scheduled work shifts and need better coordination between workforce planning and transportation operations. It is especially useful when service changes throughout the day, multiple employees can drive, coverage must be distributed fairly, or managers need to explain what happened after the fact.

RoutePulse is focused on internal shuttle operations. It is not positioned as a consumer ride-booking marketplace or a replacement for every fleet telematics platform.

Employee shuttle software FAQs

What is employee shuttle management software?

It is software that helps an organization plan, coordinate, and document transportation for employees. RoutePulse specifically connects employee work schedules to shuttle demand, driver coverage, live operations, and driving records.

Can RoutePulse use workforce schedules to plan transportation demand?

Yes. RoutePulse imports workforce schedule data and uses employee start and end times to show when transportation demand is expected during the operating day.

Can a manager change a driver assignment?

Yes. Managers can override planned coverage while RoutePulse preserves the original suggestion, the final scheduled driver, and the actual driver separately.

Does RoutePulse support QR and NFC?

Yes. The driver checkpoint supports QR and NFC workflows for recording actual driving activity, including starts, pauses, resumptions, and endings.
