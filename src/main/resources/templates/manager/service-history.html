How to read this report

Scheduled demand counts employee arrivals in the morning, arrivals plus departures at midday, and departures in the evening. It is not a passenger count. Rider confirmations count submissions, not unique people.

A review flag marks a completed demand window with meaningful scheduled demand and no recorded driving or rider evidence. Optional rider scans do not prove a missed trip. Closed days, unverified calendars, unavailable schedules and reviewed cancelled/excused/missed assignments are not automatically flagged.

Archived schedules show their retained period totals. Demand timing at period boundaries cannot be reconstructed exactly, so archived totals alone are not used to flag coverage gaps.

Period times follow the planning windows and expand to include their demand buckets. Some boundary windows overlap; report totals remove duplication. Recorded time comes from driver check-in/check-out activity, not GPS tracking.
