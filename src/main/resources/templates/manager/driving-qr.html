QR & NFC setup

Drivers can tap the NFC tag or scan the QR code. Both use the same Is the Shuttle Running driving workflow and the signed-in account identifies the driver.

Primary method

NFC tap

Program one NFC tag with this URL and place it inside the shuttle. A tap opens the same state-aware driver workflow as the QR code.

URL to write to the NFC tag https://istheshuttlerunning.com/driver/tap?code=example
The tag identifies this organization's shuttle checkpoint. It does not authenticate a driver. The driver must still be signed in to their own Is the Shuttle Running account.
  1. Copy the NFC URL above, including the entire checkpoint code.
  2. In your NFC-writing app, choose Write, add a website/URL record, paste the URL, and write it to the tag.
  3. Have each driver sign in on their own phone. They can select “Keep me signed in” on a personal device.
  4. Tap the tag, open the phone's link notification if shown, and choose “Confirm start driving” during an eligible driving window.
  5. Check for the saved-start message and verify the actual session in Driving records before mounting the tag.

Opening the link shows the action; confirming it saves the driving record. Drivers need an internet connection and must use the same browser they signed in with. Private browsing does not keep their sign-in.

Backup method

QR scan

Keep this QR posted next to the NFC tag. Drivers can scan it whenever NFC is unavailable or inconvenient.
